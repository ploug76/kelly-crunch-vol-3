# Kelly Crunch Vol. 3

Kelly is back, now as a little giraffe with a stubby neck that stretches up to grab critters! The Gobble King crashed the Rainbow Feast and carried off every pie, so Kelly sets out across five painted worlds to win it back, one critter chain at a time.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kelly-crunch-vol-3/

The first game is still here, unchanged: https://ploug76.github.io/kelly-crunch/

- 5 worlds (Sunny Garden, Misty Swamp, Sunset Canyon, Crystal Caverns and Starry Sky Castle), 30 stages: feasts, puzzles and a boss at the end of each world
- 5 bosses: Bramblejaw, Queen Bogsnap, Rockjaw, Glimmergulp and the Gobble King. Every critter you pop blasts the boss, and each boss fights back with thorns, slime, rumbles, freezes or all four
- All 230 puzzles from the first game in the Puzzle Tower, plus Endless mode
- Painted art, parallax, particles, glow and screen shake, a story intro and ending, and an original WebAudio score with a track for every world and boss
- Controls: on phones, swipe and hold to move Kelly, and tap or swipe up to grab and spit. On a keyboard, use the arrows or A/D, Space, B to burp, U to undo and H for a hint. Game controllers (USB or Bluetooth) work through the Gamepad API.
- Free to play. Works offline once it has loaded (PWA).
